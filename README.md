## Selected work

### Graph-rationale-guided RAG for medical question answering
Developed a research framework combining UMLS–Neo4j knowledge graphs, multi-source retrieval, hallucination detection, and QLoRA. Evaluated on MedQA, MedMCQA, and RareDisease-MedQuAD. The project reported 10–12% higher accuracy, approximately 20% lower hallucination rates, and over 80% graph fidelity.

Published in *Academic Journal of Science and Technology*, Vol. 19, No. 2 (2026), pp. 321–331. Developed through an online LLM research seminar supervised by Dr. Pavlos Protopapas. Metrics reflect the research results described in my résumé; this is not a clinical product.

### Xi’an tourism knowledge graph
Processed **1,000 Ctrip travel notes** into **477 analytical samples**, using entity extraction, synonym normalization, FP-Growth, and sequential pattern mining. Produced **335 association rules** and **1,448 sequential patterns**; deployed a Neo4j graph with **35 nodes and 460 directed relationships**.

Graduation thesis at Xidian University, supervised by Dr. Lei Sun · October 2025–June 2026.

### WNBA team management optimization
Led Python coding, data processing, and visualization for **2026 MCM/ICM Problem D — Honorable Award**. Supported roster optimization under salary-cap constraints using EWC, EASV, TBR, and ILP components, with scenario and sensitivity analysis. The modeled second-superstar scenario projected 30–40% valuation upside, a model estimate rather than an observed return.

### AI teaching assistant
Built a RAG assistant on the UnivAI platform with **LangChain, Haystack, reranking, and Streamlit**, retrieving course materials to answer recurring student questions. Online LLM research seminar · June–August 2025.
